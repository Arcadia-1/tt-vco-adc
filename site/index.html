Circuit Architecture & V2 Upgrades

Three Surgical Fixes Over Baseline S13

Each half cascades an OTA-C integrator and 7-stage ring VCO for true second-order (+40 dB/dec) noise shaping and intrinsic first-order DEM. Deep verification eliminated three latent silicon risks.

01 · FRONT-END NOISE

Common-Centroid Low-1/f 5T OTA

Upsizing the NMOS input pair 18× in gate area to 48/3 µm (A A B B B B A A) and PMOS load 4× to 16/2 µm cuts 1/f noise variance 14.4× while lifting DC gain to 47.8 dB and Tian PM to 72.2° across PVT.

74.6 → 22.9 µVrms (+10.2 dB SNR ceiling)
02 · ANTI-LATCHUP

5-FET Miller Brick-Wall Clamp (CLAMP_A5)

Above 0.75 fs = 7.5 MHz, XOR phase differentiation aliases backward and latches the ring near 8.5 MHz. Our zero-static-power two-stage active clamp leaks only 14–49 nA at 1.15 V yet hard-caps f_VCO ≤ 6.66 MHz with ≤ 0.32 mV ringing.

0.00 dB noise/HD3 penalty · 4/4 PEX PASS
03 · FEEDBACK INTEGRITY

Dedicated buf_4 Digital Pin Isolation

Separating external 1–5 pF pad/mux loads from internal dfxtp_1 + xor2_1 nodes via abutted buf_4 buffers eliminates back-to-back XOR glitch charge theft and rise/fall DAC asymmetry, lifting sf flat-PEX HD3 by +11.5 dB.

19–90× less glitch · 20–215× less asym

# Off-chip phase-difference decoding (test/decode_vco_adc.py)
# Bit order within the 7 sampled phases does not matter:
yP[n] = popcount(PHP[n] ^ PHP[n - 1])   # 0..7 (mean 3.5 @ mid-scale)
yN[n] = popcount(PHN[n] ^ PHN[n - 1])   # 0..7 (mean 3.5 @ mid-scale)
y[n]  = yN[n] - yP[n]                   # differential 2nd-order output
